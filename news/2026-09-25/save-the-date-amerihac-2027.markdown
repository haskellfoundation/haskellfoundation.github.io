The Haskell Foundation is happy to announce that it will again host [AmeriHac in 2027](/events/2027-amerihac.html), in collaboration with Jane Street.

The event will take place in **New York City**, on **February 6^th^ and 7^th^, 2027**. Registration will open in the coming weeks.
