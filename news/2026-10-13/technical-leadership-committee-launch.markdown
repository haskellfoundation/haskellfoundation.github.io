---
title: Launching the Technical Leadership Committee
---

Following the [call for applications to the Technical Leadership Committee](/news/2026-08-05/technical-leadership-committee.html), the Haskell Foundation is proud to present the formed committee. Our inaugural Technical Leadership Committee is composed of:

* Julian Ospald (Chair)
* Simon Marlow (Board Liaison)
* Joshua Miller
* Rodrigo Mesquita

Committee members were chosen for their demonstrated technical expertise, historical community involvement, and _bold vision_ for the future of Haskell. Head on over to the [Technical Leadership](/technical-leadership) page to learn a little bit more about them, and stay tuned for updates from the Technical Leadership Committee in the near future!
