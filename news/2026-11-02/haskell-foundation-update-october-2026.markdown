## The Technical Leadership Committee has launched

The Haskell Foundation launched the Technical Leadership Committee, a new committee of volunteers that decide how the Haskell Foundation’s resources should be spent on technical projects.

[Our inaugural committee](/technical-leadership) is composed of Haskell veterans: Julian Ospald, Simon Marlow, Rodrigo Mesquita, and Joshua Miller. Together, they form an opinionated, proactive committee that’s going to experiment more and shake things up.

## The Cabal exactprint project crosses major milestones

The Cabal exactprint project is one of two major technical endeavors supported by the Haskell Foundation today.

As a reminder, the Cabal exactprint project will give cabal-install the ability to modify Cabal files while preserving formatting and comments, a feat which will enable many tooling improvements, from formatting to full management of dependencies in Cabal files.

Exactprinting is a technical tour-de-force. Leana Chiang and Jappie Klooster have managed to get [their proposal accepted](https://github.com/haskell/cabal-proposals/pull/7), and demonstrate that their implementation can parse-and-exactprint 95% of Cabal files on Hackage.

The next milestone involves pushing exactprinting capabilities up to 99% of Cabal files on Hackage.

## www\.haskell.org is now managed by the Foundation

[Following a vote of the Haskell.Org Committee,](https://github.com/haskell-org/committee/pull/16) the Haskell Foundation is now responsible for www\.haskell.org. This includes infrastructure, but also content.

If you are interested in getting involved with www\.haskell.org, please see [our call for applications to a new working group](https://discourse.haskell.org/t/call-for-applications-to-the-haskell-org-working-group/14910).
