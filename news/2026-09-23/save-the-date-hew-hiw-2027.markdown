title: Save the date for the Haskell Ecosystem and Implementors' Workshops 2027
---

The Haskell Foundation is happy to announce that it will again host two workshops as a prelude to [ZuriHac in 2027](https://zfoh.ch/zurihac2027/), in collaboration with the OST Eastern Switzerland University of Applied Sciences:

* [Haskell Ecosystem Workshop on June 10^th^ 2027](/events/2027-haskell-ecosystem-workshop.html)
* [Haskell Implementors' Workshop on June 11^th^ 2027](/events/2027-haskell-implementors-workshop.html)

This will lead into ZuriHac, from June 12^th^ to June 14^th^. **Registration for these events will open at a later date**.
