For the new Haskell Interlude, we sat down with Edward Kmett. Ed is
the Founder and Chief Scientist of Positron AI. More importantly, he's
a legend in the Haskell community for authoring many, many popular
Haskell packages, first and foremost lens - and generally turning up
the abstraction level to 11. We talk about design principles for
Haskell libraries, category theory, cache-oblivious algorithms and his
current work involving Haskell, C++, and FPGAs. Ed talks fast, so
strap in!
