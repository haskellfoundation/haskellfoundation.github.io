In this Haskell Interlude, we sat down with Tom Schrijvers, the head
of the Functional Programming team at KU Leuven. He has been a
prolific contributor to the foundations of Haskell, with many papers
pushing the boundaries of the Haskell type system, programming with
effects, and applications of functional programming.Our conversation
was a whirlwind tour through Tom's long and wide publication history,
with lots of tangents and ideas about functional programming, logic
programming, constraints, DSLs, composition, program calculation, and
other great stuff.
