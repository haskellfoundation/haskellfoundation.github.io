Partnerships

The Haskell Foundation's Ecosystem Partnerships program connects organizations with service providers who maintain and improve critical Haskell tools and infrastructure. Through Ecosystem Contracts, organizations can fund targeted open-source development that meets their needs while supporting the long-term sustainability of the Haskell ecosystem. These partnerships benefit organizations, service providers, and the wider Haskell community by fostering sustainable investment in shared infrastructure. Interested in becoming an Ecosystem Partner? Reach out at contact@haskell.foundation.
