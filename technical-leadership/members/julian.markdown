Julian started his open source journey as a Gentoo Linux developer, working with large teams of distributed volunteers. He picked up Haskell when a friend told him about this strange new language that would revolutionize all of software engineering. This spark turned into a mission to fix tooling around Haskell (e.g. GHCup) and contribute to the larger ecosystem. His professional journey has taken him across fintech startups (Capital Match), banks (Standard Chartered Bank) and software labs (IOG).

Julian believes in open source, human collaboration and unix principles. He draws his passion from the beauty of the craft and the need to support and exercise good engineering.
