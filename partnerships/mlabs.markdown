Clients hiring [MLabs](https://www.mlabs.city/) through this partnership are also supporting the Haskell Foundation and the Haskell ecosystem.

## Offering 1

Senior Haskell engineers placed inside your team for a quarter or longer, working in your codebase and your process. Teams use this when a hire is months away and the work can't wait.

## Offering 2

Nix-based build and development infrastructure for Haskell projects: reproducible builds, CI, and developer environments that behave the same on every machine.

## Offering 3

Cardano smart contracts written in Aiken, Plutarch or Plinth. MLabs designs and builds them, and audits contracts written by others.

## Offering 4

A review of a Haskell codebase your team has stopped trusting, whether from age, turnover or AI-generated code, ending in a plan your team can carry out.
